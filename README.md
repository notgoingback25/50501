# 🔍 50501: Transparency Archive

**This is the public repository of legal filings, governance agreements, and primary documents related to the 50501 movement and its organizational conflict.**  
All files here are published to support truth, transparency, and historical accountability.

---

## ⚠️ Purpose of This Archive

The 50501 movement began as a decentralized protest initiative rooted in resisting authoritarianism and organizing across all 50 states. As the movement grew, key legal steps were taken to protect its name, infrastructure, and integrity. In April 2025, internal tensions over structure, representation, and authority led to a major breakdown—one that culminated in disputed trademark filings, Reddit admin intervention, and competing public narratives.

This archive is not intended to escalate conflict. It exists solely to preserve a public, verifiable record.
